## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Impact / likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Interactive HUD steals focus during insertion, or never accepts clicks/text due to passive panel flags. | High / high | Show feedback after successful insertion; scope click policy to feedback, use a native focused correction presentation, test target caret and keyboard manually. |
| reset/hide, permission polling or stale dismissal races remove new feedback/recording, or callbacks save an obsolete draft. | High / high | Separate completed presentation lifetime from recording task cleanup; bind callbacks to attempt identity/generation; dismiss drafts on replacement/hide and cover obsolete callbacks. |
| Rule appears saved but is truncated/ignored at parser/storage limits, or conflicting aliases apply unpredictably. | High / high | Validate whole pair and full serialized result before mutation; deterministic upsert/rejection policy; parse round-trip proof plus limit/conflict tests. |
| Glossary overwrites manual terms or feedback persists full transcript/ratings unnecessarily. | High / medium | Update current glossary on MainActor, preserve unrelated lines, persist only explicit pair through existing key; no new history/analytics, no extra copies of transcript. |
| Installed PR #9 and parallel PR #13 are confused with main or inadvertently integrated. | High / medium | Pin analyzed SHA; record installed provenance and PR states separately; no integration of those PRs; verify eventual candidate source/binary independently. |

Overall risk: **medium-high**, concentrated in native focus/callback lifetime and safe glossary serialization. Work is fully local and requires no new service, paid API, analytics, login or dependency. Privacy evidence must distinguish existing one-time model download from forbidden transmission of audio/transcript/feedback.
