# SPEC-019 — Store presence and brand asset system

## Positioning

Resenha is the free, private voice input for Mac: press, speak, release, done. The page leads with the real workflow, not generic AI claims.

## Required asset system

- Master vector mark and wordmark; protected spacing and minimum-size rules.
- App icon source plus every macOS asset-catalog size: 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 px.
- Monochrome/template menu-bar mark at 1x/2x/3x working sizes.
- Light, dark, single-color and transparent-background logo exports.
- Ten 16:10 Mac screenshots at one accepted resolution, localized in pt-BR and en-US where text differs.
- One optional 15–30 second landscape app preview built from real screen capture, with a deliberate poster frame and no fabricated behavior.
- Press/GitHub kit: SVG, PDF, PNG, favicon/social preview and concise usage guidance.

## Product-page copy

- Name at most 30 characters and distinct from existing apps.
- Subtitle at most 30 characters.
- Promotional text at most 170 characters.
- Description at most 4,000 characters.
- Keywords at most 100 bytes and without competitor/company names.
- Support URL includes real contact information; marketing and privacy URLs resolve publicly.
- Copy is localized for pt-BR and en-US, describes no unavailable feature and says "free" without implying permanence Apple cannot verify.

## Screenshot narrative

1. Hold the shortcut and speak in any compatible text field.
2. Watch the real voice waveform and elapsed time.
3. Release to transcribe locally on Apple Silicon.
4. Receive text at the cursor and recover it from local history.
5. Personalize shortcut, language and sounds.

Remaining frames show privacy, permissions, mixed-language dictation, error recovery and the compact menu. Every frame must use the shipping build and remain legible at Store thumbnail size.

## Acceptance

Assets pass dimension/alpha checks, icon small-size inspection, light/dark contrast checks and metadata byte limits. No screenshot contains private data, developer tools, fake reviews, pricing claims or unshipped UI.
